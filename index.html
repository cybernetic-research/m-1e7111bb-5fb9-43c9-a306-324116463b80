Controls

In the headset: point and pull the trigger to choose · ◀ BACK and ⌂ HOME are always there · at life size, walk in your room; push the right stick forward to aim a jump and let go to jump; left or right to turn · B or Y is back · ☰ MENU has smooth movement, music and the debug tools.
On screen: click to choose · drag the map to slide it, wheel to zoom, ← → (or Q / E) to turn it · Backspace back · Home key home · at life size drag to look, ↑ ↓ forward and back, ← → turn; or W / S forward and back, A / D step sideways, Q / E turn · Shift to hurry, click to jump.
