PROJECT M

Contour maps and 3D terrain on your real table, with today's map or the c.1900 OS map draped over them, and life size to walk around. Troopers Hill, Clifton, Clevedon, Birnbeck, Brean Down, Glastonbury Tor and the Cadbury hillforts in 1 m LIDAR.

AR: point + trigger / pinch the buttons · pinch the map with one hand to slide it, two hands to zoom and turn · stick zooms and turns · click a stick to bring the map to you · A/X next place · B/Y flat ↔ 3D · LIFE SIZE then point where to stand
Life size: left stick walk (click to hurry) · right stick turn · point + trigger to jump there · B/Y back to the table
Screen: drag the map to slide it, wheel to zoom, Q/E turn, drag elsewhere to orbit · [ ] place · 0 reset · R flat/3D · G rings · M map · O water · T now/then · L life size (then drag to look, WASD walk, Q/E turn, click to jump, Esc back)
